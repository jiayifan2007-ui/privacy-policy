Privacy Policy

Last Updated and Effective Date: July 26, 2026

Welcome to Leo&Tiger ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy strictly complies with the guidelines set forth by the Apple App Store and Google Play Store to ensure transparent handling of your data.

By downloading, accessing, or using the Leo&Tiger mobile application (the "App"), you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy.

1. Data We Collect and How We Use It (Data Safety Declaration)

To provide you with a transparent understanding of our data practices, we have detailed exactly what data we collect, whether it is mandatory or optional, and the specific purposes for processing it:

  • Name and Email Address Mandatory
    Purpose: Account Management and App Functionality. We require this to create your secure account and communicate critical service updates. If you use Apple/Google Sign-In, we only receive your basic name and email; we never access your passwords.
  • User IDs (Internal Account ID) Mandatory
    Purpose: Account Management and App Functionality. An internal Firebase unique identifier generated to securely link your device to your loyalty profile and data.
  • Financial Information (Purchase & Points History) Mandatory
    Purpose: App Functionality. To operate our loyalty program, we must record your transaction history and track your points accumulation and redemptions.
  • Other Information: Date of Birth & Dietary Preferences Optional
    Purpose: Personalization and App Functionality. Users can choose to provide this data to receive personalized rewards (e.g., birthday promotions) and tailored food recommendations (e.g., Vegan, Vegetarian). You can use the app's core features without providing this data.
  • Device or Other IDs (Push Notification Tokens) Optional
    Purpose: App Functionality. We collect Expo device tokens to send promotional or order-related push notifications. This is strictly opt-in via your device's operating system settings, and you can disable it at any time.

2. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We DO NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties under any circumstances.

We share necessary data solely with trusted infrastructure providers required to operate the App:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure user authentication, real-time database hosting, and cloud storage.
  • Expo: Used for the secure routing and delivery of push notifications to your device.

3. Data Security and Processing

We implement commercially reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data. All data in transit is fully encrypted using HTTPS/SSL protocols. We process and store this data persistently as long as your account is active; it is not processed ephemerally.

4. Data Retention and Your Deletion Rights (Crucial)

You retain full ownership of your personal data. We keep your information only as long as your account is active.

Right to Delete: You have the absolute right to request the complete and irreversible deletion of your account and all associated personal data.

  • In-App Deletion: You can permanently delete your account directly at any time by navigating to Profile > Settings > Delete Account within the App. This action immediately deletes your Firebase Authentication profile and Firestore database records.
  • Web/Email Deletion Request: Alternatively, you may request full data deletion by emailing us at the address provided below. We will process and complete your request within 14 days.

Consequences of Deletion: Please note that deleting your account will result in the immediate and permanent loss of all accumulated loyalty points, transaction history, and profile data from our active databases.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
